# 3 · Print the case

Files and settings: [`print/README.md`](../print/README.md). Short version:

1. Print `back_shell_standard.stl` (rim down) and `front_bezel_standard.stl` (front face down) in PETG: 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 30 % gyroid, no supports.
2. Print `key_caps_x2.stl` and `key_cap_primary.stl` (accent colour), flange down, and `side_buttons_x2.stl` flat.
3. Optional: `bumper_rugged.stl` + `button_covers_rugged.stl` in TPU; `usb_flap.stl` in TPU.

## Check

- The bare PCB drops onto the four standoffs of the back shell and the M2 × 5 screws bite.
- Key caps slide through the bezel holes without catching; the flange stops them.
- The two halves close with the O-ring cord in the groove (try it before you have electronics inside).
- A USB-C cable plug reaches fully into the counterbore (with the board fitted in step 6).
