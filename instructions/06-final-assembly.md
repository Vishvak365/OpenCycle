# 6 · Final assembly

1. **Lens:** apply the black mask to the inside of the 1.0 mm lens (screen window + the two small windows top-right). Glue the lens into the bezel pocket (thin bead of clear silicone or 3M VHB strip on the ledge).
2. **Display:** stick the 0.5 mm foam strips around the display frame; place the display face-down into the bezel window; the FFC tail exits at the bottom.
3. **Keys:** drop the three key caps into the bezel holes from the inside (accent cap on the right).
4. **Vent and speaker:** put the ePTFE sticker over the 0.8 mm vent inside the right wall of the back shell and the acoustic mesh over the speaker grille; set the speaker (cone toward the grille) on its foam in the pocket at the bottom of the shell. Solder its leads to LS1 on the board (red to "+").
5. **Battery:** place the Adafruit 258 in the floor pocket, leads toward the bottom. Plug it into J2 **after** checking polarity again.
6. **Board:** fold the display FFC 180° behind the panel and insert it into J3 (contacts toward the board), close the latch. Lower the board onto the standoffs (display side up) and fix it with 4 × M2 × 5.
7. **Side buttons:** insert the two plungers from the inside of the back shell.
8. **Seal and close:** lay the 1.2 mm O-ring cord in the groove (glue the ends), press the bezel onto the back shell, fix it with 4 × M2 × 6 through the corner posts.
9. Clip it into a Garmin quarter-turn mount and check it locks. The mount boss is an approximation — if it's loose, adjust `MOUNT_*` in `cad/params.py`.
