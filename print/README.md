## Settings that worked in the model

- 0.2 mm layers, 0.4 mm nozzle, 4 walls, 5 top / 4 bottom layers, 30 % gyroid.
- Shells: no supports needed. If your printer struggles with the 10 mm USB bridge, enable supports only for that opening.
- TPU: 20–25 mm/s, no retraction tricks needed.
- Check fit before assembly: the PCB drops onto the four standoffs, the key caps rise freely in the bezel, the side plungers move 0.25 mm and click the switches.

Estimated material: ~14 cm³ (back) + ~5 cm³ (bezel) + keys ≈ 25 g PETG per standard case.
